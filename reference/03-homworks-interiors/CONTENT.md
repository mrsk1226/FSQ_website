## 1. Materials — [USED: all]

- **BWP plywood (Boiling Water Proof), IS 710 / IS 1734** — hardwood core, bonded with
  phenol formaldehyde resin; high density; excellent screw-holding; resistant to
  stain, scratch and impact; termite, borer and fungus resistant.
- Construction emphasis: "100% in-house", "branded materials", precision cutting /
  edge banding discipline.

## 2. Finishes catalogue — [USED: all]

Wood finishes, acrylic finishes, matte laminates, high-gloss acrylics, quartz countertops,
glass backsplashes. Hardware/accessories: minimalistic handles, touch-to-open mechanisms,
profile shutters. Style range: minimalistic to maximalist.

## 3. Quality process — [USED: all]

  clone's Erode/Trichy showrooms).

## 7. Window / door types + hardware — [USED: all]

**Windows**
- Inventa Casement: twin gasket, multipoint lock, thicker mullion, large fixed glass; best for noisy streets
- Inventa Sliding: max reinforcement space, 66% opening, wind-resistance booster bars, minimum vertical separator
- Inventa Tilt & Turn: Z sash, tilt+turn hardware (Roto and GU), 100% inward opening
- Optima Casement: small–medium openings, 100% opening
- Optima Sliding: small–medium, 1500×1500 @ 1500 Pa

**Doors**
- Inventa Casement doors: inside/outside opening, internal & balcony, max 1200 mm width per leaf,
  toughened/laminated glass for safety, optional threshold, international hardware compatible
- Inventa Sliding doors: 3-track 3-shutter or 3-track 6-shutter options, floor-to-ceiling, large balcony doors
- Inventa Slide & Fold bi-fold: 100% opening, weather tightness comparable to casement; high rises/villas

**Hardware:** multipoint locking (2–3 points), non-corrosive SS 304 friction stays, sliding rollers,
double-bevel chamfer profiles. Glazing: single/double/triple options; double glazing keeps home cooler in
summer, warmer in winter; reduces interior fading and condensation. 15–20% of wall surface is typically windows.

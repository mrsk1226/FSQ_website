# Product Image Specifications & Asset Requirements

This document specifies the exact photographic asset requirements for every public uPVC and Aluminium product route on the Four Square website.

---

## 1. uPVC Windows & Doors

### 1.1 uPVC Casement Windows
- **Route**: `#/products/upvc/casement`
- **Product**: Inventa & Optima Casement uPVC Windows
- **Required Image**: High-resolution studio/architectural photo of an openable casement window installed in a contemporary luxury residential interior.
- **Ideal Angle**: 3/4 perspective showing one open sash (approx. 45° swing) and one fixed/closed sash.
- **Frame Colour**: Contemporary Anthracite Grey or Golden Oak finish.
- **Opening Type**: Side-hung open-out casement with visible stainless friction hinges.
- **Orientation**: Landscape (16:9 or 4:3).
- **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 px minimum, 72 dpi WebP/PNG.
- **Generation / Sourcing**: Sourcing from Four Square site installations or high-fidelity 3D studio render.

### 1.2 uPVC Sliding Windows
- **Route**: `#/products/upvc/sliding`
- **Product**: Inventa & Optima Multi-Track Sliding uPVC Windows
- **Required Image**: 2-track or 3-track horizontal sliding window with visible multi-track rail and overlapping sashes.
- **Ideal Angle**: Frontal 15° slight oblique showing clean track grooves and smooth sash offset.
- **Frame Colour**: Standard Architectural White or Sheffield Oak Grey.
- **Opening Type**: Horizontal multi-panel sliding.
- **Orientation**: Landscape (16:9).
- **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 px minimum.
- **Generation / Sourcing**: Sourced from actual completed residential project.

### 1.3 uPVC Tilt & Turn Windows
- **Route**: `#/products/upvc/tilt-turn`
- **Product**: Inventa Tilt & Turn German Hardware Window
- **Required Image**: Tilt & Turn window sash tilted top-inward (15° draft-free ventilation position) in an upscale bedroom or high-rise living space.
- **Ideal Angle**: Interior eye-level 30° perspective highlighting the top-tilt clearance and perimeter dual-action handle.
- **Frame Colour**: Pristine White or Walnuss Amaretto.
- **Opening Type**: Dual-action (Top tilt inward / side swing inward).
- **Orientation**: Landscape (16:9 or 4:3).
- **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 px minimum.
- **Generation / Sourcing**: Studio demonstration photography or precision 3D architectural render.

### 1.4 uPVC Casement Doors
- **Route**: `#/products/upvc-doors/casement`
- **Product**: Inventa Heavy Casement uPVC Door
- **Required Image**: Full-height glazed terrace or balcony single/French door with rebated low threshold.
- **Ideal Angle**: 3/4 standing angle showing tall vertical glass proportions, multi-point handle, and 3D adjustable barrel hinges.
- **Frame Colour**: Dark Oak, Mahagoni, or Standard White.
- **Opening Type**: Outward swing glazed door.
- **Orientation**: Portrait or tall Landscape (4:5 / 16:9 wide environment).
- **Resolution**: 1920 × 1440 px minimum.
- **Generation / Sourcing**: Villa balcony / garden entrance shoot.

### 1.5 uPVC Sliding Doors
- **Route**: `#/products/upvc-doors/sliding`
- **Product**: Inventa Panoramic Sliding Patio uPVC Door
- **Required Image**: Large floor-to-ceiling 3-track sliding door opening onto a landscaped terrace or deck.
- **Ideal Angle**: Wide architectural interior-to-exterior view showcasing extensive daylight and minimal floor transition.
- **Frame Colour**: Sheffield Oak Grey, Black Cherry, or Golden Oak.
- **Opening Type**: Multi-panel horizontal sliding patio door.
- **Orientation**: Landscape (16:9).
- **Resolution**: 2560 × 1440 px (Retina / 4K ready).
- **Generation / Sourcing**: Project photography of villa patio installation.

### 1.6 uPVC Slide & Fold Doors
- **Route**: `#/products/upvc-doors/slide-fold`
- **Product**: Inventa Bi-Fold Concertina uPVC Doors
- **Required Image**: 3 to 5-panel concertina folding door in partially folded/stacked state creating an uninterrupted wide opening.
- **Ideal Angle**: 45° dynamic perspective showing folding hinges, top guide track, and stacked sashes.
- **Frame Colour**: Oregon Pine Natur, Golden Oak, or Anthracite Grey.
- **Opening Type**: Accordion concertina bi-fold.
- **Orientation**: Landscape (16:9 or 21:9 ultra-wide).
- **Resolution**: 2560 × 1440 px minimum.
- **Generation / Sourcing**: High-end showroom or completed villa living-to-pool deck install.

## 2. Aluminium Systems

### 2.1 Aluminium Sliding Systems (Graf 26 / 32 / 45)
- **Route**: `#/products/aluminium/sliding`
- **Product**: Graf Series Architectural Aluminium Sliding Systems
- **Required Image**: Ultra-slimline aluminium sliding doors with monumental glass panels, flush floor track, and crisp metallic profile.
- **Ideal Angle**: Eye-level interior view capturing both the slim vertical interlocking mullions and expansive landscape view outside.
- **Frame Colour**: Powder-coated Noir, Slate, or Anodized Champagne.
- **Opening Type**: Multi-track architectural sliding.
- **Orientation**: Landscape (16:9).
- **Resolution**: 2560 × 1440 px.
- **Generation / Sourcing**: Luxury penthouse or architect-designed residence shoot.

### 2.2 Aluminium Casement Systems (Livio Minimal & Robus 40)
- **Route**: `#/products/aluminium/casement`
- **Product**: Livio Minimal & Robus 40 Casement Aluminium Windows
- **Required Image**: High-performance slim aluminium casement window with flush sash profile and concealed friction stays.
- **Ideal Angle**: 3/4 angled close-up highlighting clean mitered corner joints, flush sash lines, and architectural hardware.
- **Frame Colour**: Urban Dusk, Noir, or Satin Natural Silver Anodized.
- **Opening Type**: Side-hung casement / Awning top-hung.
- **Orientation**: Landscape (16:9).
- **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 px.
- **Generation / Sourcing**: Architectural facade or interior photography.

- **Required Images**: Powder-coat satin & wood-grain finish swatches (500 × 500 px, 1:1) for:
